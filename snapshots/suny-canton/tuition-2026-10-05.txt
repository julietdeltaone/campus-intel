<p>To learn more about this exciting new program and how you might be eligible to attend SUNY Canton tuition-free:</p>

<h2 class="bluebar">Additional Notes</h2>
<h3>Roo Express Account</h3>
<p>A Roo Express account is an account funded by your loans/financial aid package, to use at the Campus Center Store, dining facilities, vending machines, eCampus (off campus textbook vendor) and local off-campus businesses that accepts Roo Express. A maximum of $1,200 per semester may be requested. <strong>The Student Accounts Office has the ability to lower the amount of or cancel your request, to process a bill if you do not have enough funds to cover the requested amount.</strong></p>
<p><strong>To request additional Roo Express, after your bill has been processed, send an email to <a href="mailto:billing@canton.edu">billing@canton.edu</a></strong><strong> from your SUNY Canton e-mail.</strong></p>
<p>You will access your ROO Express account with the student ID card. You may also add funds to your ROO Express account by adding funds to the mobile app called GET Mobile or on the web at <a href="https://get.cbord.com/sunycanton">get.cbord.com/sunycanton</a>.</p>
<h3>Campus Cash Account</h3>
<p>This is an on-campus spending account. Campus cash may be spent at all dining locations, Campus Center Store, and vending machines. Campus Cash is part of your meal plan and is non-refundable.</p>
<p>To adjust Roo Express when utilizing financial aid after your bill has been processed, send an email to <a href="mailto:billing@canton.edu">billing@canton.edu</a>&nbsp;from your SUNY Canton e-mail.</p>
<p>This account is accessed with the student ID card. You may also establish this discretionary spending account, separate from your financial aid package, online at <a href="https://get.cbord.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">get.cbord.com</a>.</p>
<h3>Hold Policy</h3>
<p>Untimely payment of outstanding student account balances may result in a hold being placed on a student's account and the subsequent withholding of enrollment, grades, diplomas and class selection. Students are encouraged to contact the Student Service Center to discuss any special circumstances surrounding an outstanding account. Balances remaining at the end of the semester are subject to referral to the New York State Attorney General or a collection agency.</p></div>
