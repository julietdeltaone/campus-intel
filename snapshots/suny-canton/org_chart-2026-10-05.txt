      <h1>Make Our Great Majors Into Your Great Career</h1>
<p>SUNY Canton makes education a hands-on engaging experience to prepare its scholars for careers.</p>
<p><img src="/media/commencement2.jpg" alt="Students taking selfies at graduation" width : 580;" height : 340;" style="" /></p>
<h2 class="bluebar">Founded in 1906</h2>
<p>The College was originally chartered in 1906 as the first postsecondary, two-year college in New York. In 1987, the State University of New York&rsquo;s Board of Trustees authorized the college's present designation as State University of New York College of Technology at Canton, more commonly known as SUNY Canton.</p>
<p><img src="/media/walking.jpg" alt="Students walking through the plaza after class." width : 580;" height : 340;" style="" /></p>
<h2 class="bluebar">Academics</h2>
<p>SUNY Canton offers:</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="/academics/bachelors.html">30 career-driven bachelor's degrees</a></li>
<li><a href="/academics/associate.html">20 associate degrees</a></li>
<li><a href="/academics/certificate.html">3&nbsp;one-year professional certificate programs</a></li>
<li><a href="/ol/">25 online degrees</a></li>
<li><a href="/academics/micro/">30+ microcredentials</a></li>
</ul>
<p>Areas of study fall into one of three unique schools:</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="/csoet/">The Canino School of Engineering Technology</a></li>
<li><a href="/business/">The School of Business and Liberal Arts</a></li>
<li><a href="/sci_health/">The School of Health, Science and Criminal Justice</a></li>
</ul>
<p>All academic programs are designed in a ladder curriculum, meaning students who start in a certificate or associate program can apply their first years of study toward their associate or bachelor&rsquo;s degree.</p>
<p><a href="/ol/">SUNY Canton OnLine</a> features more than 450 online courses and 25 degrees are available online, allowing students to enroll from anywhere with Internet access.</p>
<p><img src="/media/academics-open.jpg" alt="A student assembles a steel bridge." width : 600;" height : 300;" style="" /></p>
<h2 class="bluebar">Student Enrollment</h2>
<ul>
<li>During Fall 2025, there were 2,800 undergraduate students</li>
<li>Student legal sex is 56% female, 44% male</li>
<li>Diverse mix of races, ethnicities and backgrounds represented in the student body, adding for a unique and rich cultural learning experience</li>
<li>International students from Canada, Cameroon, United Kingdom, Sweden, Finland, Australia, Czech Republic, Democratic Republic of Congo, Gabon, Japan, South Korea, and Sri Lanka.</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="bluebar">Faculty</h2>
<p>The College&rsquo;s faculty members are noted for their professional real-world experience in addition to their academic credentials. Students learn from engineers; crime scene investigators and former police officers; nurses and doctors; lawyers and entrepreneurs; programmers; in addition to novelists, photographers and videographers. The student to faculty ratio is 17 to 1.</p>
<h2 class="bluebar">College Leadership</h2>
<p>Dr. Zvi Szafran is the <a href="/president/">President</a>. There are four divisions led by a vice president reporting to the President.</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="/provost/">Provost/Vice President for Academic Affairs</a> - Peggy DeCooke</li>
<li><a href="/administrative_services/">Vice President for Administration/Chief Financial Officer</a> - Eric Shinnick</li>
<li><a href="/advancement/">Vice President for Advancement</a> - Geoffrey VanderWoude</li>
<li><a href="/student_affairs/">Vice President for Student Affairs/Dean of Students</a> - Courtney Bish</li>
<li><a href="/strategic/">Vice President for Strategic Relations &amp; Communications</a> - Lenore VanderZee</li>
</ul>
<p><a href="/media/pdf/Org_Chart.pdf">Organizational Chart</a></p>
<h2 class="bluebar">Athletics</h2>
<p>The college&rsquo;s 15 traditional athletic teams compete at the NCAA Division III level as part of the SUNYAC. SUNY Canton also offers varsity esports and cheerleading.</p>
<p><img src="/media/basketball.jpg" alt="A men's basketball player drives to the hole for a score" width : 580;" height : 290;" style="" /></p>
<h2 class="bluebar">Esports</h2>
<p>SUNY Canton's Esports teams offer students a chance to participate in video game competitions at the collegiate level.Options include varsity and intramural matches. The College currently participates in <a href="https://www.canton.edu/esports/#lol">League of Legends</a>,&nbsp;<a href="https://www.canton.edu/esports/#overwatch">Overwatch</a>,&nbsp;<a href="https://www.canton.edu/esports/#rocket">Rocket League</a>, <a href="https://www.canton.edu/esports/#smash">Super Smash Bros Ultimate</a>, and <a href="https://www.canton.edu/esports/#valorant">Valorant</a>. Additional games and teams are added regularly. SUNY Canton is the first college in New York to join the <a href="https://nacesports.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">National Association of Collegiate Esports (NACE)</a>&nbsp;and competes in the <a href="http://www.ecacesports.com/">Eastern College Athletic Conference (ECAC)</a>.</p>
<p><img src="/media/esports19.jpg" alt="A female gamer plays in the eSports Arena" width : 580;" height : 340;" style="" /></p>
<h2 class="bluebar"><strong>The Kangaroo</strong></h2>
<p>In 2007, SUNY Canton adopted the Kangaroos as its nickname, logo and mascot. The marsupial can only move forward, reflecting the College&rsquo;s progress and mission. The athletic teams are often called &ldquo;Roos&rdquo; and the athletic building is nicknamed &ldquo;Roos house.&rdquo; The mascot&rsquo;s name is Roody Roo.</p>
<p><img src="/media/roody.jpg" alt="Roody hangs out in the Miller Campus Center" width : 580;" height : 290;" style="" /></p>
<h2 class="bluebar">About the Campus</h2>
<p>SUNY Canton is comprised of 555 acres located in a scenic, semi-rural setting bordering the Grasse River and within walking distance of downtown Canton.</p>
<blockquote>
<ul>
<li><a href="/campus_tour/">Campus Tour</a></li>
<li><a href="/map/">Campus Map</a></li>
</ul>
</blockquote>
<p><img src="/media/kennedy-hall4.jpg" alt="Students walking to class from Kennedy Hall." width : 580;" height : 340;" style="" /></p>
<p><strong>Twenty buildings fit perfectly into the surrounding landscape, including:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Five classroom buildings with specialized labs for innovative hands-on learning</li>
<li>Five residential buildings, including Kennedy Hall, offering students single-rooms in apartment-style suites</li>
<li>The Convocation Athletic and Recreation Center features an ice rink, pool, fitness center, and field house</li>
<li>The Southworth Learning Commons, serves as the technological center and research hub of campus</li>
<li>The Richard W. Miller Campus Center offering recreational and student access space for clubs and organizations</li>
<li>Miles of beautiful trails suitable for walking, running, biking or skiing surround the campus</li>
<li>Two scenic footbridges connect the campus with the Village of Canton</li>
</ul></div>
