We work with you to make sure that there are no barriers between you and your future. Discover the many ways we can help make your college experience affordable.</p><p class="text-lg text-bold">&nbsp;</p><h2 class="text-lg text-bold">Cost of Attendance 2026-2027</h2><table class="table"><tbody><tr><td colspan="2"><strong>BILLABLE EXPENSES</strong></td></tr><tr><td><strong>Tuition</strong></td><td>$61,694</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Fees</strong></td><td>&nbsp; $1,370</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Housing (average)</strong></td><td>$10,840</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Food</strong></td><td>$8,528</td></tr><tr><td><strong>TOTAL DIRECT COSTS:</strong></td><td><strong>$82,432</strong></td></tr><tr><td colspan="2"><strong>ADDITIONAL ESTIMATED EXPENSES</strong></td></tr><tr><td><strong>Books, Course Materials, Supplies, Equipment</strong></td><td>$1,560</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Personal</strong></td><td>$900</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Transportation</strong></td><td>$1,596</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Loan Fees</strong></td><td>$68</td></tr><tr><td><strong>TOTAL COSTS OF ATTENDANCE:</strong></td><td>$86,556</td></tr></tbody></table><h3>Housing Costs</h3><p>Housing costs are based on a weighted average. Freshman housing is usually slightly less than the weighted average. See costs and financial aid for international students for more information.<br><br><a class="button button--router button--full" href="https://www.clarkson.edu/admissions-aid/international/tuition-costs">View Costs and Financial Aid for International Students Here</a></p></div>
